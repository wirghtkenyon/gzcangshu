FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

A quick reference for the questions we hear most often. For anything else, the contact form above gets to a human quickly.

Do you take on client projects?

We focus on our own product line — six apps on the App Store. We don't currently take on contract work, but we partner selectively with aligned organisations. Reach out if you'd like to discuss.

Where are your apps available?

Our apps are available worldwide through the Apple App Store. We support iOS 15+ and iPadOS 15+. We're exploring Android, web, and other platforms based on demand.

How do you handle user privacy?

Local-first is our foundation. Data stays on your device by default; cloud sync (where available) is end-to-end encrypted with keys only you control. We collect zero analytics telemetry. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.

Are you hiring?

We're not actively hiring at the moment, but we're always interested in connecting with talented engineers and designers who share our philosophy. Send your portfolio to contact@gzcangshu.com.
